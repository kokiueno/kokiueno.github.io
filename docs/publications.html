Research

Papers & challenges

Uncertainty in generative forecasting, and post-training robot foundation models so they hold up on real hardware.

01 Papers

Uncertainty-Guided Iterative Decoding for Diffusion-Based Time Series Forecasting (UGID)

IJCAI 2025 · AI4TS Workshop · 2025

Diffusion forecasters commit to early denoising steps they are not confident about. UGID uses per-step uncertainty to decide where to iterate, improving accuracy without retraining the base model. Extended version under review at Transactions on Artificial Intelligence.

02 Challenges

Post-Training π0.5 for Bimanual Manipulation on the YAM Robot

RSS 2026 · Post-Training for Robotics Foundation Models Challenge · 2026

HIL-aware data curation plus reward-weighted SFT, using a VLM as the reward model. Presented the technical report and poster and joined the panel discussion at the RSS 2026 workshop in Sydney.
