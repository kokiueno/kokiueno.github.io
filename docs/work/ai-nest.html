Client delivery at AI Nest

  • AI Nest Inc.
  • Tokyo, Japan (hybrid)
  • Apr 2025 – Present
  • Executive Officer, Principal Research Engineer

Three roles: project manager on client engagements — end to end, not one stage of them — presales, and product manager for our own products.

01 Three roles

Project manager

Client engagements end to end. Not one stage of the project but the whole span of it, and whatever that turns out to require.

Presales

Before an engagement exists: hearing what the client is struggling with, then proposing what is technically possible against it — usually with a demo, since a proposal they can watch working beats one they have to read. The same engagements then carry through to delivery.

Product manager

Development management for our own products, alongside the client work.

02 End to end, in practice

Hearing the problem, proposing the answer

Listening to what the client is struggling with, then proposing what is technically possible against it — often by building a demo rather than a document.

Requirements

Turning that into something buildable and checkable: what the system must do, what counts as working, and what is deliberately out of scope.

Building it

Hands on the code, not just the plan. Designing, validating and shipping the system rather than handing a specification to someone else.

Managing to delivery

Owning scope, roadmap and the schedule the client is actually holding you to — the work that decides whether a good prototype becomes a delivered system.

03 What the systems are

RAG & LLM agents

Retrieval-augmented generation and agent systems, including an enterprise file search system and an AI gateway delivered with its AI security assessment.

Vision

Image-based systems, where the useful signal is in what a camera sees rather than in text.

Physical AI

AI that acts on the physical world rather than staying inside a screen.

AI process automation

Automating the work itself: the internal processes that quietly consume a team's week, handed to AI where that genuinely holds up.
