04 PCB simulation & field prediction

At AT&S, semiconductor R&D. Printed circuit boards are modelled in FEM, but a solver run is slow and only as good as the parameters it is given. I worked the other way round as well — feeding measured data back in, and learning the fields the solver would have produced.

Measurements into the model

Engineered the bench measurement data into features the FEM setup could actually consume — cleaning, aligning and deriving the quantities that pinned the simulation to the boards in front of us.

Heat & current distribution

Predicted where heat and current concentrate across the board, and the trajectories they follow through traces and vias — the hot spots and crowding that decide whether a layout survives.

Learned surrogate

An electric potential trajectory model trained to stand in for the process simulation, returning a field estimate in a fraction of the time a full solve takes.
