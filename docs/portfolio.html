Four lines of work. Much of it sits behind a corporate NDA, so what follows is the shape of the problem rather than the code. Cards marked “Case study” open a fuller write-up.

01 Robot foundation models

Vision-Language-Action manipulation policies. Six threads run through it:

Onto the production line

Taking policies from benchmark tasks to a real manufacturing line — where the failure modes are physical, not statistical.

Human data into policy learning

Putting data collected from people to work in policy training, rather than leaving it to robot demonstrations alone.

Agent explainability

Making the agent's behaviour explainable — why it did what it did, not just whether it worked.

Human safety via flow models

Reasoning about the safety of people around the robot through flow models.

VLA with reinforcement learning

Reinforcement learning applied to VLA — pushing past what imitation alone gives you.

World action models (WAM)

Predicting what the world does next, so a policy can plan against it instead of reacting to it.
